# CliniVol designer workflow

- Scope and mode: desktop web operational prototype spanning Home, task detail, and reconstruction workspace; Operate.
- Audience and job: reconstruction designers need to locate assigned cases, verify context, resume a professional workspace, save, submit, and respond to review without losing task identity.
- Primary action: complete and submit a reconstruction task for physician review.
- Content and constraints: synthetic patient/order data; no real imaging algorithm, AI segmentation, CAD, FEA, 3D print, hospital, sales, or admin expansion.
- Direction: a bright, restrained TDesign platform shell hands off to a compact Windows-style professional workspace. Menus, tools and side panels remain light; only the four image viewports are dark.
- Memorable moment: submitting turns the live workspace into a visibly locked review version, and Home immediately reflects the new status.
- Unresolved: whether multi-task tabs should remain in the final architecture is intentionally left for UX validation.
